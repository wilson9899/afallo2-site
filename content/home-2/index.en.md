# About AllO2

AllO2 International Co., Ltd  
began as a specialized team dedicated to tree cultivation and species research, focusing on fast-growing species such as Paulownia, Albasia, Tremula, Cadamba. Through years of hands-on experience, we recognized that traditional reforestation efforts, while valuable, were insufficient to counter the accelerating impacts of climate change and ecosystem degradation. In response to this urgent need, we expanded our mission to develop innovative, nature-based carbon solutions that address both environmental and community challenges.

In 2022, AllO2 International Co., Ltd  
proudly introduced our brand, AllO2, symbolizing our commitment to uniting everyone on Earth to fill the world with oxygen—an action for all, O2. This vision reflects our belief in collective action to restore balance to the planet.

Operating in Southeast Asia, a region particularly vulnerable to the impacts of climate change, we understand the immense challenges of identifying and accessing suitable land for carbon projects. Our flagship ARR project in Oktwin Township, Bago Region, Myanmar, stands as a testament to our capability in navigating these complexities, from land acquisition to project execution. This project not only highlights our technical expertise but also showcases our dedication to achieving tangible, verifiable outcomes.

By combining scientific rigor, sustainable land management, and close collaboration with local communities, AllO2 strives to foster biodiversity, restore ecosystems, and address climate challenges. We remain committed to supporting the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) while empowering vulnerable communities across the region.As discussions around coastal resilience continue to gain momentum, AllO2 has extended its expertise into the blue carbon domain. We are currently working with local partners in Mozambique to conduct feasibility assessments for mangrove restoration initiatives. This new development marks a strategic step from forest-based carbon sinks toward coastal ecosystem restoration, further expanding our practical application of nature-based solutions on a global scale. Through our on-the-ground experience and growing portfolio of carbon projects, AllO2 is leading the way in delivering measurable climate solutions where they are needed most.

Come join us in breathing life into our planet through the power of photosynthesis and collective action.

## AllO2 Consultant

![](/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/林幸助4.jpg)

Blue Carbon Consultant Ph.D. Hsing Juh Lin

Taiwan Lifetime Distinguished Professor  
National Chung Hsing University

Professor Hsing-Juh Lin, a Lifetime Distinguished Professor at National Chung Hsing University, is an internationally acclaimed authority on wetland ecology and blue carbon. He serves as a Lead Author for the UN’s Regional Assessment Report on Biodiversity and Ecosystem Services for Intergovernmental Science-Policy Platform on Biodiversity and Ecosystem Services (IPBES).  
He is a member of the IUCN’s Mangrove Specialist Group, where he leads the Red List of Ecosystems Assessments for Mangroves. With over 200 peer-reviewed publications, his expertise centers on ecosystem services and carbon sequestration. As Taiwan’s first carbon credit technical expert certified by the TAF, his profound academic background and extensive international project experience provide the critical scientific oversight necessary to ensure the success of global mangrove carbon credit initiatives.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/林士淵2.jpg)

Land & Geospatial Consultant Ph.D. Lin Shih Yuan

Department of Land Economics, National Chengchi University

Professor Lin Shih-Yuan is a full-time professor in the Department of Land Economics at National Chengchi University. His expertise includes geospatial land information analysis, remote sensing applications, and land-use change research. His work integrates GIS, remote sensing technologies, and spatial econometric methods, with a long-term focus on cross-border land-use structures and the integrated analysis of spatial data. He also has the capability to transform multi-source geospatial information into standardized land interpretation models.  
Together with AllO2, Professor Lin has been developing a cross-border remote sensing and land interpretation framework. This framework systematically integrates satellite imagery, geospatial data, and local ground-truth information to build a consistent and scalable spatial data structure, which is being accumulated over time as a reusable long-term data asset. The objective is to provide a critical foundation for MRV (Measurement, Reporting, and Verification) in nature-based carbon sequestration projects, thereby enhancing data accuracy and transparency.
