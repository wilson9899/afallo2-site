# Impact Investors

We welcome private equity funds and mainstream corporations to join our reforestation and carbon reduction initiatives. Our services are tailored to your specific investment goals, whether they focus on financial returns or achieving SDG objectives. Here are the key steps to get started.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/n-icon-01-1-1.svg) Provision of specific development requirements

We begin by understanding your investment goals, whether focused on financial returns, achieving SDG targets, or both. Together, we will define project objectives based on your priorities.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/n-icon-02-1-1.svg)Discussion and agreement finalization

Upon completing and signing the engagement agreement, we will begin identifying suitable investment projects. The research timeline will depend on the project scale, ranging from two weeks to six months.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/n-icon-03-1-2.svg)Potential investment report

We will provide a detailed investment report outlining potential projects and suitable implementation methods. You can decide whether to entrust project execution to us or a designated nonprofit organization.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/NEW_icon-05.svg) Project implementation plan and certification process (optional service)

We offer project implementation based on the terms of the agreement. Once entrusted, we will execute the project, ensuring all necessary steps are taken to achieve your defined goals.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/n-icon-05-1-2.svg) Execution of planting or conservation projects with carbon credit issuance (optional service)

We assist with managing, selling, or utilizing carbon credits or timber products generated from the project, as specified in the agreement.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/n-icon-06-1-1.svg)Project closing and final settlement (optional service)

Upon project completion, we can assist with final settlement, including carbon credit issuance, timber acquisition, or profit distribution, in accordance with the agreed terms.
