        ## What We Do

        AllO2 specializes in land-use assessments and AFOLU carbon credit project evaluations across Southeast Asia. In addition to consultancy services, we are actively executing our own ARR project, delivering climate solutions that prioritize community well-being and sustainability while addressing local needs.

        ### **Consulting 1Services**

        AllO2 specializes in carbon credit projects within the Agriculture, Forestry, and Other Land Use (AFOLU) sector, providing expert consulting in forest management, land-use strategies, and carbon offset advisory. Our services include applying rigorous scientific methodologies to measure carbon sequestration and evaluate ecological impacts, delivering practical solutions that align with international standards for businesses and landowners.

        ### Project 1Development

        We support landowners and land-owning companies in developing Verra VCS carbon credit projects. Our comprehensive services cover every stage, from initial land assessments and project design to data measurement and verification. Leveraging advanced scientific methodologies and transparent data management, we ensure each project meets international standards, providing traceable and verifiable carbon offset solutions.

        [ View more ](/en/what-we-do/)

        ## Our Project

        AllO2 is deeply committed to advancing natural carbon sink solutions throughout Southeast Asia. Our flagship ARR project in Oktwin Township, Bago Region, Myanmar, serves as a cornerstone of our efforts. Looking ahead, we are evaluating several promising carbon project opportunities to expand our impact. Through these initiatives, we strive to achieve net-zero emissions while fostering biodiversity, restoring ecosystems, and working closely with local communities to support the SDGs.

        By fostering collaboration with local communities, our initiatives promote biodiversity conservation and environmental restoration while contributing to the achievement of the SDGs. AllO2 remains committed to achieving net-zero emissions by 2050, leveraging innovative solutions to combat climate change.

        ## Sustainability in Action: AllO2's Commitments

        Our Myanmar ARR project aims to drive sustainable development through the cultivation of Paulownia tomentosa, focusing on creating significant social and environmental impact. By providing employment opportunities, particularly for women, and fostering skill development for both youth and adults, the project contributes to local economic growth. It ensures inclusivity by implementing policies that prevent discrimination and sexual harassment, promoting a fair and safe working environment. Additionally, the project focuses on increasing water-use efficiency, reducing water scarcity, and encouraging sustainable building practices. AllO2’s project aligns with global sustainability goals, contributing to both environmental restoration and improved community well-being.
